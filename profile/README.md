## SabiVida mini apps

The official developer home of [SabiVida](https://sabivida.com), the household life-admin service for Cape Verdean families on the islands and across the diaspora. *Vive sábi, djuntu.*

A SabiVida mini app is a partner's web app that opens inside SabiVida for consular appointments, travel, money transfers, schooling, community life and more. SabiVida signs the member in, and the member can add the partner's next step to the household's plans with one confirmation.

### How it works

- **Invited partners.** Every app is reviewed before members see it. There's no open store.
- **Consent first.** Each app gets its own member id, and the member's name only if they choose to share it. Household data, documents, plans, chat and contacts never leave SabiVida.
- **Visible trust.** SabiVida's bar above every app shows its host, and the public [registry](https://github.com/sabivida-mini-apps/registry) lists every genuine partner.
